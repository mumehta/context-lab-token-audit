# Context Lab

## Register
product

## Users
People using Claude Code, Codex, and ChatGPT who need to understand unexpectedly high usage.

## Product Purpose
Diagnose context waste through a self-report audit, explore repetition, and choose evidence-backed fixes.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, practical. The user selected a practical diagnostic tool for everyday AI users.

## Anti-references
No marketing gate before the tool, fake account telemetry, or universalized provider claims.

## Design Principles
Expose uncertainty; separate products; connect evidence to action; keep simulation assumptions visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, semantic landmarks, text severity labels, readable contrast, responsive layout, reduced motion.
